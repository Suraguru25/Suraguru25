### 👥 Customer Retention & Cohort Analysis

**PostgreSQL · SQL · CTEs · Window Functions**

Analysed **805K+ purchase lines** from 5,878 identifiable customers (UCI Online Retail II) to study repeat purchasing, cohort retention, purchase timing and revenue.

**Key findings**
- **71.3%** of customers made repeat purchases
- Median **63 days** to the second purchase day among returning customers
- About **21%** Month 1 retention
- Repeat customers contributed **96.0%** of gross revenue (GBP 16.74M of GBP 17.43M)

**Recommendation:** test re-engagement campaigns between days 30 and 90, and compare cohort retention patterns to improve repeat purchasing.

🔗 [View Repository](https://github.com/Suraguru25/Customer-Retention-Cohort-Analysis-SQL)
